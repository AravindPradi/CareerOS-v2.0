# Product Requirement Document (PRD) - CareerOS

## 1. Executive Summary
**CareerOS** is an AI-powered Career Operating System designed to help job seekers globally maximize their employment outcomes. Moving beyond traditional job boards and simple resume generators, CareerOS guides users through a continuous 8-phase career lifecycle: **Self-Assessment → Resume Building → ATS Optimization → Job Discovery → Application Tracking → Interview Preparation → Offer Management → Career Growth**.

---

## 2. Core Vision & Value Proposition
- **Outcome-Driven Guidance:** Focuses on measurable conversion improvements (e.g., +40% interview shortlist rate, +60% ATS pass rate).
- **Daily Companion:** Active engagement via the **Career GPS** daily mission engine.
- **Global Mobility:** Specialized **Migration Path Generator** for international employment across top European destinations.
- **Automation & Efficiency:** **One Click Apply Pack** reduces application prep time from 45 minutes to under 60 seconds.

---

## 3. User Personas
1. **The Active Job Hunter (Recent Graduate / Mid-Level):** Needs speed, volume, high ATS match rates, and structured tracking.
2. **The International Aspirant:** Seeking work visas, relocation insight, and country eligibility analysis in countries like Germany, Poland, Netherlands, Czech Republic, Slovakia, and Lithuania.
3. **The Career Transitioner:** Requires skill-gap analysis, resume refactoring, and AI mock interview prep to switch industries.

---

## 4. Flagship Differentiator Features

### 4.1 Career GPS (Daily Mission Engine)
- **Concept:** Gamified daily career tasks based on profile readiness and application status.
- **Daily Missions:**
  - Apply to 5 targeted roles.
  - Elevate ATS resume score by +10%.
  - Complete 1 AI Voice/Text Interview Simulation.
  - Optimize LinkedIn headline for current algorithm parameters.
- **Metrics:** Daily Streaks, Career Progression Score (0-1000), Gamified Level Badges.

### 4.2 Rejection Intelligence Engine
- **Concept:** Automated pattern detection from rejected job applications.
- **Root Cause Classification:**
  - Missing Hard/Soft Keywords (e.g., AWS, React 19, Kubernetes).
  - Skill Gaps relative to seniority level.
  - Weak ATS Score (<70%).
  - Salary Expectations mismatch.
  - Work Visa / Country Eligibility restrictions.
- **Deliverables:** Automated Root Cause Analysis Report + 5-step corrective Action Matrix.

### 4.3 One Click Apply Pack
- **Concept:** Instant application package generator triggered by pasting any Job URL or Job Description.
- **Generated Pack Items:**
  1. ATS-Optimized Master Resume (PDF & Text).
  2. Official Europass CV (Formatted XML/PDF).
  3. Tailored Cover Letter.
  4. Personalized HR Cold Email / LinkedIn DM Draft.
  5. Predicted Role-Specific Interview Questions & Model Answers.
  6. Instant Skill Gap Report.
- **Export:** Single ZIP download or individual file export.

### 4.4 Why Not Me Analyzer
- **Concept:** Side-by-side gap breakdown between candidate resume and target Job Description.
- **Output Metrics:**
  - Match Percentage (0-100%).
  - Missing Critical Keywords Heatmap.
  - Experience Gap & Seniority Alignment.
  - 3 High-Impact Fixes to instantly boost match rate by 15-30%.

### 4.5 Migration Path Generator
- **Target Destination Countries:** Poland, Germany, Czech Republic, Lithuania, Slovakia, Netherlands.
- **Calculations & Insights:**
  - Visa Pathway & Sponsorship Probability (EU Blue Card, Skilled Worker Visa, D-Type National Visa).
  - Estimated Relocation & Monthly Living Costs (Rent, Tax, Utilities).
  - Target Role Salary Expectations (Local Currency & EUR).
  - Country-Specific Eligibility Score (0-100%).

## 5. Comprehensive Dashboard Modules (14 Modules + Admin)

1. **Career Overview & Daily GPS:** Dashboard summary, progress widgets, active daily missions, streak status.
2. **ATS Center:** Real-time resume scanning, keyword density analysis, formatting compliance checker, score breakdown.
3. **Resume Builder:** WYSIWYG live editor, AI bullet point generator, modern sleek templates, PDF/Word export.
4. **Europass Generator:** Standard European Union CV layout builder with multi-language export.
5. **Job Finder:** Aggregated job discovery engine with remote filters, visa sponsorship indicators, and instant match scoring.
6. **Job Tracker:** Interactive drag-and-drop Kanban application pipeline (Saved → Applied → Interviewing → Offered → Rejected).
7. **Interview Center:** Interactive AI Mock Interviewer with voice/text feedback, speech pace evaluation, and scoring.
8. **AI Career Coach:** Multi-turn conversational AI companion trained on salary negotiation, career pivot strategies, and resume critique.
9. **Skill Gap Analysis:** Heatmap comparing current skills against market demand for target role; recommended courses.
10. **Migration Planner:** International job mobility matrix, cost of living index, visa calculator for 6 target European countries.
11. **LinkedIn Optimizer:** AI analysis of profile headline, summary, experience bullet points, and recruiter search visibility.
12. **One Click Apply Pack:** Paste URL workflow producing bundled application assets.
13. **Rejection Intelligence:** Application failure analytics, systemic gap identification, action plan generator.
14. **Subscription & Billing:** Transparent tier comparison, Razorpay checkout integration, payment history.
15. **SaaS Admin Operations Panel:** Multi-tenant overview, user activity logs, revenue analytics (MRR/ARR), ATS scan usage charts, country demand map.

## 6. Authentication & Security
- **Auth Protocols:** JWT (Access + Refresh tokens), Google OAuth 2.0, LinkedIn OAuth 2.0, Mobile OTP, Email OTP.
- **Security Standards:** 2-Factor Authentication (2FA), CSRF protection, AES-256 encrypted stored documents, OWASP Top 10 compliance, IP-based Rate Limiting.
